Everything you need to know about Play Pickleball NOW! — from getting started to scoring your first tournament.

Play Pickleball NOW! is the app for the person who runs the pickleball group. Set up your group and roster once, generate round-robin or fixed-team matchups in seconds, score live across everyone's phones, and let the app keep the rankings, podiums and head-to-head records. Play Now beacons let you tell nearby players "I'm at the court — come play."
