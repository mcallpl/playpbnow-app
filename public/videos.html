Quick Start

Everything an organizer needs for the first match:

  • Make a group and paste in your list of names
  • Tap M or F for each player
  • Build a Mixed match: never two men against two women
  • Repeat partners show in orange, and a tap swaps them
  • Keep score, or just play
  • Name your courts (CRT D, E, F) with one tap
  • Text the lineup to everyone's phone
