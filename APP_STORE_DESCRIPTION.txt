DESCRIPTION
================================================================================
PlayPBNow is the essential app for pickleball players who want to keep score,
track statistics, and compete with friends.

KEY FEATURES:

• Quick Match Scoring
  Create matches in under 30 seconds and tap to record scores in real-time.
  No complicated setup needed.

• Live Leaderboards
  See your ranking and your friends' standings instantly. Track wins, losses,
  and win percentage across all your matches.

• Group Management
  Create groups with a home court, build the roster, and track group
  statistics. Build your pickleball community and compete together.

• Smart Matchups
  Rotating partners or fixed teams, mixed or gender rounds, round robin
  with playoffs. The app builds who plays who; you just play.

• Head-to-Head Statistics
  Compare detailed stats with other players. See match history, win records,
  and competitive matchups.

• Beacons
  Tell players you're at the court and want to play. Anyone who opens Play
  Now nearby can see your beacon, claim a spot, and chat.

• Live Shared Scoring
  Share a 6-letter code and everyone at the court scores from their own
  phone, in sync, in real time.
